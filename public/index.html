Startup Opportunity Engine

Enter a market or customer group. This searches recent public discussions, extracts the problems people actually describe, groups them, and ranks them by how well the evidence supports them — with every conclusion traceable back to the post it came from.

Searches Reddit, Hacker News, and GitHub issues from the last 30 days. A run takes 1–3 minutes.

What these sources can and cannot tell you

Reddit, Hacker News, and GitHub are heavily weighted toward developers, tech workers, and early adopters. They are a good window into software and technical tooling, and a poor one into most other markets — trades, healthcare, logistics, hospitality, professional services. If the people in your market do not post there, this tool will tell you it found too little rather than invent something.

    What this score is, and what it is not

    Evidence Strength measures one thing: how well supported a problem is by the sources searched — how many distinct people raised it, how much the community engaged, how recently, and across how many different sources.

    It is not a prediction that a business will succeed. It knows nothing about market size, willingness to pay, competition, technical feasibility, regulation, timing, or whether you are the right person to build it. A high score means "people really are saying this", not "this is a good idea".
